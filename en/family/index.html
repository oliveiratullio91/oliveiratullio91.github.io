Family guide — Matetic

Guidance on privacy, time limits, breaks and family supervision.

Last updated: October 2, 2026 Contact: valionsuport@gmail.com

Updated November 5, 2026. For version 1.1.0 and later after publication. Versions through 1.0.45 have no ads or real-money in-game purchases.

Audience and access

Matetic is intended for ages 13 and over. Download becomes free after the store price change. Google Play provides the regional content rating. Select the real age group; do not choose an older group to unlock features. Under-13 and undisclosed-age choices do not unlock play in this version; previous progress is preserved.

Players aged 13–17 play locally without ads, real-money purchases, online accounts or public leaderboards. Adults need an adult confirmation from Google Play to use online services and monetization. Without confirmation, local play remains available.

Coins and purchases

Purchases are optional. Earn coins through play and redeem Freeze ×5 for 450 coins or Eliminate ×5 for 550 coins. Eligible adults with a permanent account can also buy these consumable packs at the price shown by Google Play before payment. No subscriptions or paid randomized items. Use Google Play purchase authentication and supervision tools. The Matetic PIN neither replaces these controls nor verifies parental consent.

Eligible adults can receive a non-personalized home-screen banner after applicable privacy steps. No ads during questions or mandatory rewarded videos. When required, “Ad privacy” lets users revisit their choices.

Usage and breaks

Open Settings > Game or Account > Guardian area. Only foreground usage today and during the last seven days is stored on the device. Teen profiles start with a 30-minute daily limit, a product default rather than medical advice. A guardian can create a six-digit PIN and select 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes.

The game pauses at the limit. Changes require the PIN and preserve usage already recorded. Switching accounts does not reset time. Access to controls expires after five minutes, leaving the area or backgrounding the app. Five incorrect PIN attempts cause a one-minute wait. Keep the PIN safe: no email recovery. Clearing app data or uninstalling can erase progress and local controls.

Agree on schedules, take breaks and alternate with offline activities. Daily play is unnecessary. Minor profiles have no daily-attendance bonuses. Use a nickname without a full name, phone number or address. There is no chat.
